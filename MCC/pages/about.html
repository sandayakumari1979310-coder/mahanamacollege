About Mahanama College

A premier national school in the Uva Province providing holistic education since 1907.

History of the School

Mahanama College was first established in 1907 as a Pirivena on land owned by Shri Sudarmananda Temple under the first principal, Ven. Obbegoda Thanhankara Thero.

In 1925, under an educational project led by Srimath Anagarika Dharmapala and Sir Henry Olcott, the school moved to Wiharamulla and was upgraded to a government mixed school under Principal Mr. A. H. V. Aththanayake.

In 1960, it relocated to its permanent site on Pottuvil Road as a Maha Vidyalaya under Mr. D. N. Vanduragala, and achieved 1 (AB) National School status in 1993.
